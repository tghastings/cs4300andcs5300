\section{Purpose}

Every team pitches the idea it submitted in Sprint 0-0. After all pitches have been heard, each team submits its ranked preferences for which project it wants to build.

You are not guaranteed the project you pitched. Ranking happens across the whole class, and assignments are made by the instructor. From this point forward, the application you build is not necessarily the application you personally proposed---and getting comfortable with that is most of the point of this course.

\section{Detailed Requirements}

\subsection{In-Class Project Pitch (60 pts)}

Present the project your team proposed in Sprint 0-0. Keep it short---you are selling an idea, not defending a thesis. Cover:

\begin{itemize}
    \item the problem, and who actually has it;
    \item what you intend to build, in concrete terms;
    \item the key features that make it worth a semester of work;
    \item where the third-party API and the database fit; and
    \item the single thing you are least sure about.
\end{itemize}

Every team member should speak.

Remember that your audience is the rest of the class, and they are about to rank these projects. A pitch that nobody understands will not be chosen by anybody.

\subsection{Preference Form Submitted (40 pts)}

Listen to every pitch. Then, as a team, submit your ranked preferences using the course preference form:

\begin{center}
\url{https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSeymzxvTjTgHDyH1ws_BYBMco2uub3IIT1kaJs7dO7tkDfCLw/viewform}
\end{center}

The form asks for:

\begin{itemize}
    \item your team number;
    \item your 1st choice project;
    \item your 2nd choice project; and
    \item your 3rd choice project.
\end{itemize}

Submit \textbf{one response per team}, not one per student. Decide on your ranking together before anyone opens the form.

Rank three \textbf{distinct} projects. Submitting fewer than three choices, or ranking the same project more than once, reduces the instructor's ability to honor your preferences---and a team that does not submit preferences is assigned a project without them.

\section{Submission Requirements}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Deliver your pitch in class on the day this sprint is due.
    \item Submit the preference form once, on behalf of your team, after all pitches have been heard.
\end{itemize}

There is no Canvas document for this sprint. The form submission is the deliverable.

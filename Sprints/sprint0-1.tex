\section{Purpose}

Every team pitches the idea they submitted in Sprint 0-0. After all pitches have been heard, each team submits its ranked preferences for which project it wants to build.

You are not guaranteed the project you pitched. Ranking happens across the whole class, and assignments are made by the professor. The application you build is not the application you personally proposed.

\section{Detailed Requirements}

\subsection{In-Class Project Pitch (60 pts)}

Present the project your team proposed in Sprint 0-0. Keep it short---you are selling an idea, not defending a thesis. Cover:

\begin{itemize}
    \item the problem;
    \item what you intend for the development team to build, in concrete terms;
    \item the key features that make it worth a semester of work;
    \item where the database, the third-party API, and the AI API fit; and
    \item the single thing you are least sure about.
\end{itemize}

Every team member should speak and remember that your audience is the rest of the class, and they are about to rank these projects. A pitch that nobody understands will not be chosen by anybody and your project will lose points.

\subsection{Preference Form Submitted (40 pts)}

Listen to every pitch. Then, as a team, submit your ranked preferences using the course preference form in Canvas.

The form asks for:

\begin{itemize}
    \item your team number;
    \item your 1st choice project;
    \item your 2nd choice project; and
    \item your 3rd choice project.
\end{itemize}

Submit \textbf{one response per team}, not one per student. Decide on your ranking together before anyone opens the form. Rank three \textbf{distinct} projects. Submitting fewer than three choices, or ranking the same project more than once, reduces the instructor's ability to honor your preferences.

\section{Submission Requirements}

\begin{itemize}
    \item Deliver your pitch in class.
    \item Submit the preference form once, on behalf of your team, after all pitches have been heard.
\end{itemize}

There is no document to write for this sprint. The pitch and the preference form submission are the deliverables.
